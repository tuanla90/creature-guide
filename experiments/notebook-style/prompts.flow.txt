An open field research notebook lying flat, photographed from directly above, two facing pages filling a 16:9 landscape frame, the centre gutter visible. The paper is cream waterproof field-book paper with a faint pale blue 5 mm grid, slightly wavy from damp; a dried mud fingerprint on one corner and a small pressed fern leaf taped at the outer edge. The drawings are graphite pencil and fine black ink by a trained field biologist: one large study of a small squat four-legged amphibian-like creature with a single closed plant bulb on its back, like a large onion bulb with visible leaf layers, wide head, large eyes, short sturdy legs with three claws, three-quarter view, loose construction lines and light cross-hatching; three smaller detail studies drawn apart and isolated on the page: the leaf layers of the bulb seen from above, a three-toed footprint beside a plain scale bar, and the ring where the bulb grows out of the back. One single accent colour, a thin cyan-blue coloured pencil, used only to trace fine paths of energy running from the bulb down into the body. Large areas of blank paper left around the drawings for notes. No writing, no letters, no numbers, no arrows anywhere on the pages. Soft even daylight, realistic photograph of a real notebook. Avoid: text, handwriting, labels, logo, watermark, cartoon, anime, colour other than graphite, black and the one cyan-blue accent.
